The **Watchlists** section in the sidebar replaces the old Calendar entry. It
has three tabs:

| Tab | Purpose |
| --- | ------- |
| **Watch Next** | Your personal queue of the next episode to watch for each started show |
| **Upcoming** | Release calendar with the same date window, search, filters, and download links as before |
| **My Lists** | Custom lists you create, rename, reorder, and delete |

<Callout type="info">

Bookmarks to **Calendar** (`/dashboard/calendar`) still work — they redirect to
**Upcoming** inside Watchlists.

</Callout>

## Private ownership

Every watchlist belongs to the account that created it. Other users cannot see
your lists, your Watch Next row, or your watched checkmarks. Deleting a user
removes their lists and viewing activity together.

## Custom lists

From **My Lists**, click **Create list** (or **New list** in the tab bar) and
give the list a name. Open a list to rename it, add a description, reorder
items, or delete the list.

Add media from any show, movie, or episode detail page with **Add to
Watchlist**. The same title cannot be added twice to one list. Lists can hold
movies, whole shows, or individual episodes together.

### Show entries advance automatically

When a list contains a **show**, the row always points at the next downloaded,
unwatched episode. After you finish that episode — through playback or by
marking it watched — the row updates to the following episode without re-adding
the show.

## Watch Next

**Watch Next** is separate from custom lists. It shows at most one row per show you
have started watching, ordered by recent activity. A show appears after you begin
an episode (playback progress) or add the show to a list.

Each row offers **Play** / **Resume** and a watched toggle for that episode.
When the current episode is marked watched or completes during playback, the row
advances to the next unwatched episode for that show.

<Callout type="info">

Watch Next is per user. Clearing your lists does not clear Watch Next until viewing
activity for those shows is gone.

</Callout>

## Watched state

Watched checkmarks on shows, seasons, episodes, and movies are **global for your
account** — the same state appears on detail pages, custom lists, and Watch Next
regardless of list membership.

- **Automatic completion** — finishing playback marks the movie or episode
  watched.
- **Manual override** — use **Mark watched** / **Mark unwatched** anywhere the
  toggle appears. Manual choice overrides automatic completion until you change
  it again.
- **Batch actions** — show and season menus can mark every downloaded episode at
  once.

Watched state does not remove items from your lists; it only affects progress
display and what Watch Next considers "next".

## Upcoming

**Upcoming** keeps the release calendar: pick a date range, sort ascending or
descending, search by title, filter by media type or download status, and open
linked shows or movies. Nothing about those controls changed when Calendar moved
under Watchlists.

## Clearing viewing activity vs deleting lists

| Action | Where | Effect |
| ------ | ----- | ------ |
| **Clear viewing activity** | My Account | Resets watched state and playback progress; **lists are kept** |
| **Delete list** | List detail | Removes only that list; library files and watched state stay |

Use **Clear viewing activity** when you want a fresh progress slate without
rebuilding your lists.
